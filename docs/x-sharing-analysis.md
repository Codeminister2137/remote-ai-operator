# X / community sharing analysis (2026-11-03)

## What is known (public sources)

- Bonus prizes total **up to $50,000** for standout projects or community
  achievements such as a **public vote or the most viral project**,
  announced on or about **Nov 10**. (Rules coverage, Oct 25.)
- Main judging runs to **Nov 8**; winners announced at a live final **Nov 9**.
- Entries score: impact 30%, creative Meta-tool use 25%, demo incl. video
  25%, technical depth 20%.
- No public mechanic found for the vote/viral bonus: no confirmed hashtag,
  platform, voting page, or eligibility window. The owner-pasted event guide
  and the event Discord are the authoritative sources — check both before
  posting anything framed as an entry.

## Window assessment

Most likely the vote/viral measurement runs **during judging (Nov 4–8)**,
possibly extending to the Nov 10 bonus announcement. Posting at submission
(Nov 4) plus steady posts through judging week covers every plausible
window. Do not wait for Nov 10 to start.

## Feasibility without an X following

Honest assessment: a cold account rarely wins a pure popularity vote
against established builder accounts. The compensating advantages here
are real but narrow:

- A genuinely working phone-first demo (video + hosted URL) beats pitch
  threads; demos get shared on merit.
- Hackathon hashtags/Discord concentrate the exact audience that votes;
  a good entry post there reaches voters without followers.
- The open-weights (Baseten) and Modal angles give partner-adjacent
  accounts a reason to amplify.

Recommendation: compete, but cheaply — one strong submission post, one
mid-judging update, replies to every comment. Under an hour total. Do not
buy engagement, do not spam tags, do not let sharing eat build/submit time.

## Suggested assets (prepare at submit time)

1. The 3-minute video (upload first; everything links to it).
2. One 30–60s vertical cut or GIF: phone → approve → applied importer.
3. Submission post draft: problem → demo link → video → what Meta models
   did (Spark coding, STT, translate) → repo link. Keep the hosted demo
   URL and access-code instructions out of public posts unless the rules
   explicitly allow public judge access; link the video instead.
4. Mid-judging update: one technical depth point (receipts, single-writer
   volume, or the Baseten open-weights lane).
